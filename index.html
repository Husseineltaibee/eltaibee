About

Associate Professor in the Electronics and Electrical Communications Department at Tanta University, Egypt, where I have served since 2001. From September 2026 I also coordinate the Communication and Computers Engineering (CCE) program at the Faculty of Engineering, Mansoura National University (MNU). Earlier I held a joint post at Pharos University in Alexandria, including Acting Vice Dean for Graduate Studies and Research.

I earned my BSc (first in class, with honours) and MSc at Tanta University and my PhD in Electrical Engineering at King Saud University, Riyadh (2017). Between 2010 and 2017 I was a researcher at PSATRI and KACST-TIC in Riyadh, and in 2013 I spent three months as a visiting researcher at Keysight Technologies in Böblingen, Germany.
